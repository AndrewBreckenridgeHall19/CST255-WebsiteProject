I chose computer science because I am interested in learning how software, websites, and technology systems are created. I enjoy the problem-solving side of technology and want to understand how coding can be used to build useful and creative projects.

Combining Technology and Fashion

One of my long-term goals is to combine computer science with my clothing brand. I would like to create and improve my own fashion website, make the shopping experience easier for customers, and use technology to help organize products, designs, and business tasks.

I am also interested in finding a computer science internship so I can gain professional experience, improve my technical skills, and learn how development teams complete real projects.
